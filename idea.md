
# ReWear – Digital Wardrobe & Outfit Discovery

## 1. The Idea in One Sentence

ReWear helps people who own many clothes but struggle to create new outfits discover different combinations using the clothes they already have.

**Tagline:** New outfits, from your own wardrobe.

## 2. Who It Is For

- **User group:** Young adults and university students who own many clothes but often wear the same outfits.
- **Where I can find five of them:** University, student groups, friends and acquaintances.
- **Device and situation:** Mobile phone, especially when choosing what to wear before going out.

## 3. The Problem (My Hypothesis)

I believe that many people have plenty of clothes in their wardrobes but still feel they have nothing to wear.

They often repeat the same outfits because they forget about clothes they own or find it difficult to combine different pieces.

I will know if this problem is real if interviews show that people frequently struggle to choose outfits, forget about clothes in their wardrobes, or want to discover new combinations without buying more clothes.

## 4. Platform

**Platform:** iOS mobile app.

A mobile app is suitable because users can access their digital wardrobe whenever they need to choose an outfit.

## 5. Out of Scope

1. Buying and selling clothes.
2. Social networking and sharing outfits with other users.
3. Virtual try-on using augmented reality.
